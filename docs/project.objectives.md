# PricePilot AI – Project Objectives

## 1. Project Title

**PricePilot AI: Dynamic Pricing Optimization & Revenue Intelligence System**

---

## 2. Project Overview

PricePilot AI is an AI-powered web application designed to help businesses optimize product pricing and maximize revenue through intelligent pricing strategies. The system uses historical pricing data, retail pricing datasets, and e-commerce sales data to support better pricing decisions. It provides a secure platform for managing products, pricing, and business analytics while preparing the foundation for future AI-based price prediction and demand forecasting.

---

## 3. Problem Statement

Many businesses still rely on manual pricing methods or fixed pricing rules, making it difficult to respond to changing market conditions, customer demand, and competitor pricing. These traditional methods often lead to reduced profits and inefficient pricing decisions.

PricePilot AI addresses this problem by providing a centralized pricing management system with AI-ready architecture that enables businesses to manage products, update prices efficiently, and prepare for intelligent pricing optimization.

---

## 4. Project Objectives

The main objectives of PricePilot AI are:

- Develop a secure web-based pricing optimization system.
- Implement user authentication using JWT.
- Provide role-based access control for different users.
- Build product management and pricing management modules.
- Maintain product pricing history.
- Load and manage retail pricing and e-commerce sales datasets.
- Design a scalable architecture for future AI modules.
- Build a user-friendly dashboard for pricing insights.
- Prepare the system for AI-based price prediction and demand forecasting.

---

## 5. Milestone 1 Objectives

The objectives of Milestone 1 are:

- Initialize the project structure.
